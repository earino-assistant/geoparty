GeoParty

A Jackbox-style geoguessing party game. Drop a pin on the world map, watch the reveal, score by distance. No accounts, no installs, no server. Two ways to play:

Couch mode: one person drives street-level imagery from their phone while everyone else watches the TV and shouts suggestions. Open the host page on a phone, the screen page on the TV, enter the room code (or scan the QR).

Head-to-head: every team plays on its own phone — same location, everyone explores and guesses at once while the TV splits into live panels, then reveals all the pins together. The winning team's phone becomes the host for the next game. Open the head-to-head page on each phone; the first phone creates the room, the rest scan its QR.

Host Couch mode — one phone drives, the couch shouts. Head-to-Head Every team on its own phone, racing on the same location. Screen TV display for both modes — the view everyone watches.
