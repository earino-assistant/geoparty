GeoParty

A Jackbox-style geoguessing party game. One person drives street-level imagery from their phone while everyone else watches the TV and shouts suggestions. Drop a pin on the world map, watch the reveal, score by distance. No accounts, no installs, no server.

How to play: the operator opens the host page on their phone and starts a game. It shows a room code and a QR code. Open the screen page on the TV (cast a browser tab, smart TV browser, or Fire Stick) and enter the code — or scan the QR, which fills it in automatically. Then play.

Host Phone controller — drive the imagery and make the guesses. Screen TV display — the spectator view the whole couch watches.
