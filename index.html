GeoParty

A Jackbox-style geoguessing party game. Drop a pin on the world map, watch the reveal, score by distance. No accounts, no installs, no server. Two ways to play:

Couch mode: one person drives street-level imagery from their phone while everyone else watches the TV and shouts suggestions. Open the host page on a phone, the screen page on the TV, enter the room code (or scan the QR).

Head-to-head: every team plays on its own phone — same location, everyone explores and guesses at once, rival pins in plain sight, then all the pins reveal together. The winning team's phone becomes the host for the next game. Play around one table with a TV showing live panels, or entirely over the internet — no shared screen needed. The first phone creates the room, the rest scan its QR or tap the invite link.
