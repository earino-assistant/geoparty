GeoParty how to play

Five steps to your first round.

  1. 1

    Open GeoParty and pick a way to play — solo, or a party.

  2. 2

    You're dropped somewhere in the world — no map, no clues.

  3. 3

    Look around, then tap the map to drop your pin.

  4. 4

    Closer + faster = more points.

  5. 5

    Put it on the TV, or share a link to challenge a friend.
