## What triggered it
Natha (Eduardo's son) hit the game in the wild: finished a Ghost Duel run, said
"I don't see the share option" while staring at the primary button (labeled
"Challenge them back"). Proved the growth loop's engine was invisible to a
first-time user. Led to a complete content-strategy audit.

## Decisions made this session (durable)
1. **Governing principle (owner):** the cleanest word that gives the best
   affordance for our audience — casual users who play a few minutes a week.
   "Share" is the social verb (share icon + "share" = trained muscle memory for
   posting to a group chat). Consistency serves affordance, never overrides it.
2. **Duel share button = "Share your run"** (NOT "Send", NOT "Share your run
   back"). "Send" was wrong (the loop is posting to a group chat = a share);
   "back" was redundant/awkward (the user just played their run). Card line
   matches: "⚔️ Share your run".
3. **Consent/report copy** reworded (owner-approved): banner body now says the
   replay covers "menus and score screens (the street view and maps are blanked
   out)" — promise unchanged, wording honest. "Sharing is on/off", "Send one
   report about this image?", "Nothing left your phone."
4. **Structural gaps consciously deferred (owner: "too lean to even know"):**
   silent-QR-when-payload-overflows (qr.js:166) and no countdown-to-next-Daily.
   Do not re-raise unless usage grows.
